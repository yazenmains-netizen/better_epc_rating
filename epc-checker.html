What is an EPC?

An Energy Performance Certificate rates your property's energy efficiency from A (most efficient) to G (least). From 2030, all rented properties in England and Wales must be rated C or above.

⚠️

The 2028/2030 deadline

New tenancies must have EPC C by 2028. All tenancies — including existing ones — must comply by 2030. Non-compliance carries fines of up to £30,000 per property.

Why order matters

Doing improvements in the wrong order wastes money. Insulation before heating upgrades. The right sequence can save thousands. That's what our survey is designed to identify.
