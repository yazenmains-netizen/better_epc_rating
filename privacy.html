Privacy and your enquiry

Updated 3 October 2026.

Who handles your details

Better EPC Rating handles the personal information you provide through this enquiry form. Contact us at BetterEPCRating@gmail.com about your information or privacy choices.

What we collect and why

We collect your name, contact details, property postcode, address and any EPC information you supply so we can respond to your request, arrange the service and keep a record of the enquiry. We also record the campaign and advert information included in the page link to understand which adverts generate enquiries, using our legitimate interest in measuring our marketing. Enquiry handling is necessary to take steps at your request before providing a service.

Where your information goes

Enquiries are processed by our website hosting and form providers, Vercel and Modal, with address search provided by Ideal Postcodes, and stored in a Google spreadsheet. Google also provides our email service. Twilio sends an SMS acknowledgement to UK mobile numbers and reports its delivery status. These messages relate to your enquiry; they are not a marketing subscription. Access is limited to the people and service providers who need it to handle enquiries and deliver the service. We do not sell your contact details.

These providers may process information outside the UK under their applicable data-protection arrangements. You can contact us for further information about the providers and arrangements used for your enquiry.

Advertising cookies

The consultation page uses the Meta Pixel, which sets cookies to measure visits to the page and successful enquiries from our Meta adverts. You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings, or use the button below to clear them from this site; this does not affect your enquiry. We also use temporary session storage to prevent duplicate submissions and display your enquiry receipt. If you use the address search, your postcode is sent to Ideal Postcodes to list matching addresses.

Keeping your information

We retain enquiry information for as long as it is needed to deal with your request and any resulting service, complaint or record-keeping obligation. Contact us to request deletion of an enquiry that is no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to correct your details, stop contacting you, provide a copy of your information or delete it where applicable. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests or request restriction. You can clear advertising cookies at any time using the button above or your browser settings. These rights can be subject to legal exceptions.

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, contact us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
