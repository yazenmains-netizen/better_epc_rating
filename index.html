Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the EPC C deadline definitely happening?

The UK government has proposed that all private rental properties reach EPC C by 2030. While final legislation is still being confirmed, the direction of travel is clear — and landlords who act now will be best positioned regardless of exact timelines.

What is an EPC Roadmap Report?

It's a personalised report showing exactly which improvements your specific property needs to reach EPC C, in what order, at what estimated cost, and the expected impact of each measure on your rating. It's a clear, actionable plan — not just a certificate.

How does the fully remote service work?

We use your property details and existing data to model the current EPC and map out the best improvement route. No home visit needed for most properties. Once works are complete, you send us photos and we issue your updated EPC — all remotely.

How much does it cost to reach EPC C?

It depends entirely on the property — which is exactly why the Roadmap Report is so valuable. We find the cheapest combination of improvements for your specific property. Many landlords find the works are far more affordable than they expected.

My property is already EPC D — do I really need to do much?

It depends on the specific score. A high-D property might only need one or two targeted improvements to reach C. The Roadmap Report will show you exactly where you stand and the most efficient path to compliance.

Do all the upgrades need to be done at once?

Not at all. The Roadmap Report shows a priority order so you can phase improvements over time, keeping costs manageable — as long as you reach EPC C before the 2030 deadline.
